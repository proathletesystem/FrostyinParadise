Prototype history

The Story Behind FROSTY

FROSTY in Paradise™ began as a working prototype invented in Newport Beach on Balboa Island in 2000.

Now redesigned for Hawaii, it brings a playful winter photo-op into a tropical setting — creating a unique attraction for guests, families, tourists, and holiday visitors.

Not just a display. A seasonal destination.

FROSTY is designed to help high-traffic locations create holiday moments that people photograph, share, and remember.
